### Hi, I'm Jani

I'm an MSc student in astronomy and space physics at the University of Turku, with my
thesis remaining. I'm looking for roles in the space sector where I can use my
background in space physics and data analysis, such as satellite operations, mission
and ground station support, and space data analysis. I'm based in Turku, Finland, and
open to relocating.

- **Thesis:** recalibrated a particle instrument on the ESA/NASA SOHO spacecraft using
  29 years of measurements, correcting an error of up to 13 %. The corrected data are
  already in research use.
- **Space Research Laboratory, University of Turku** (2017–2018, intern and research
  assistant): I developed a Python analysis pipeline that fits a model of particle
  acceleration by solar shock waves to measurements of high-energy particles from solar
  eruptions. It replaced the group's legacy Fortran code, fitted data sets the old code
  could not, and the group adopted it. I also checked the mathematics of the group's
  simulation by re-deriving it independently. We shared the code on GitHub.
- **Project:** [satellite-pass-planner](https://github.com/jani-hilt/satellite-pass-planner)
  predicts when a satellite is in contact with a ground station. The code was written
  with an AI coding agent. I set the goals and checked the predictions by hand against
  Heavens-Above and SkySafari, for four satellites from three places.
- **In progress: CosmoSim** (since September 2026, not yet public): a Python simulation
  of gravity acting on millions of particles, validated with galaxy collisions and now
  being extended to cosmology. AI coding agents write the code; I decide what is built
  and verify the results against analytical solutions and published values.
